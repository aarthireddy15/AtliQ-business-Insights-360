## Finance view
### Objective:-
1)The main objective is to enhance the budget process.  

2)Uncover the netsales & GrossMargin so as to increase the netprofit.
### Project Outcome:-
1)Created a BenchMark against Target Verses LastYear.

but,GrossMargin% is not meeting the targets.  

3)Which is actually effecting the NetProfit.

## Sales View
### Objective:-
1)The overall intent is to increase the sales &  

2)To make sure that products are reaching large target population. 
### Project Outcome:-
1)Made a detailed analysis on which customer is reaching a wider population.

## Marketing View:-
### Objective:-

### Project Outcome:-

## SupplyChain View:-
### Objective:-
1)To enhance the inventory risk management so as to reduce the losses.

1)We have created key metrics like ForecastAccuracy% , ForecastAccuracy%LY,NetError,NetError%,Risk for customers & products.  

2)So this Forcast Accuracy helps to predict which product is out of stock & which is having excess inventory,so as to increase the profit's by reducing the costs.
