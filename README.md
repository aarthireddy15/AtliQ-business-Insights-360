# AtliQ-Business-Insights-360

## Project Outline
The project aims to simplify and enhance various aspects of AtliQ's Hardwares business like the Finance , Sales , Marketing , Supply Chain , Executive View.So that they can have a better perception of the business.
Below are the objective & Outcome of the project listed below:

### Live Dashboard Link: https://app.powerbi.com/links/LoIYTMIe2Y?ctid=b1b6682a-45d9-494f-b45d-1b81a7b32868&pbi_source=linkShare

## Finance view
### Objective:-
The main objective is to enhance the budget process &
uncover the netsales & GrossMargin so as to increase the netprofit.
### Project Outcome:-
Created a BenchMark against Target Verses LastYear.
Analysis helped to figure out that the NetSales is meeting the targets
but,GrossMargin% is not meeting the targets.
so it is actually effecting the NetProfit.

## Sales View
### Objective:-
The overall intent is to increase the sales &
To make sure that products are reaching large target population. 
### Project Outcome:-
Made a detailed analysis on which customer is reaching a wider population 
& found out that they are having high GrossMargin% ,as they are giving more discounts
so it is effecting the profits.

## Marketing View:-
### Objective:-
To ensure that the customer reaches the target population by making Data driven marketing stratergies.
Focus to increase the Brand visibility of the products by promoting them through various social media & advertising platforms.
### Project Outcome:-
By promoting the products, we can see an increase in the sales of the products generating high GrossMargin%.
Overall it is effecting customers net profit,but intially it is good for them to make market presence.
but can make slight minor changes by reducing the operational expenses.

## SupplyChain View:-
### Objective:-
To enhance the inventory risk management so as to reduce the losses.
For which we have created Forecast Accuracy.
### Project Outcome:-
We have created key metrics like ForecastAccuracy% , ForecastAccuracy%LY,NetError,NetError%,Risk for customers & products.
So this Forcast Accuracy helps to predict which product is out of stock & which is having excess inventory,so as to increase the profit's by reducing the costs.

## Executive View:-
### Objective:-
Provide an overview of the overall performance of the company.
This helps to make Data driven decisions by senior management of the company.
### ProjectOutcome:-
The overall analysis of the MarketShare of various companies helps to make insighful Decisions.
Where we have created revenue contribution by Division,channel,Subzone & Countries generating high revenue as well as revenue contribution by various products.
